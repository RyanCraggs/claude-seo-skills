# Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) / AI Visibility

How the brand shows up in AI-generated answers (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews). Frame honestly: this is early, measurement is immature, and outputs vary wildly between tools — treat it as a **directional bonus signal, not a primary KPI**.

## Inputs (from the user)

Claude can't run these — request whatever the user has:
- **AI Overview data** (Semrush/Ahrefs): keywords where the site appears in AIOs, and which pages.
- **Semrush AI Visibility:** AI Visibility score, mentions, citations, cited pages, platform breakdown, country breakdown.
- **Gumshoe.ai report:** persona-based visibility simulation (visibility rate, brand leaderboard, per-persona breakdown).
- **Custom-GPT brand-visibility report** (e.g., Aleyda Solis's AI Brand & Product Visibility GPT): how ChatGPT recognizes, describes, and recommends the brand.

Leave `[SCREENSHOT: …]` placeholders for the dashboards and leaderboards.

## Section intro (template)

> Traditional search still accounts for the vast majority of behavior, and only a small share of people currently rely on AI tools — but the shift is significant enough that it can't be ignored. This section looks at current visibility and how to improve the odds of appearing in AI-generated summaries and answers.

## What to report

**AI Overviews:** growth trend, how many keywords/pages trigger an AIO, and the top pages by AIO keyword count (table: URL · # AIO keywords · est. monthly organic traffic).

**AI search / LLMs:** whatever the tools show. If using Gumshoe, report the visibility rate, whether the brand leads the category leaderboard, and per-persona consistency. If using Semrush AI Visibility, report the score/mentions/citations/cited-pages and platform split, and note the trend direction honestly (growing or declining).

- *Strong result (mature brand):* if the brand leads its category across models, say so plainly — once models mention a brand consistently they tend to keep doing so, which compounds. Becoming the "default" AI-recommended option is a real competitive advantage.
- *Weak result (new site):* low visibility usually reflects a small content footprint and few indexable pages for AI to cite; AI leans on third-party sources instead. This is expected pre-launch and improves as the site grows.

## The two-mechanism model (the strategic core)

Always explain the two ways a brand shows up in LLMs:

1. **Getting mentioned** — the brand exists in the model's training data. Improved by appearing as often as possible in reputable, contextually relevant places online: PR, expert interviews, podcasts, third-party reviews, academic citations, health journalism.
2. **Getting cited** — the brand's live URLs appear in results the model summarizes. Improved by ranking well in traditional search — i.e., standard SEO.

**The punchline:** the long-term strategy for AI visibility is the same strategy that wins traditional visibility. Get cited widely, publish authoritative content, earn backlinks, strengthen technical and on-page fundamentals. LLM visibility isn't a separate discipline — it's the natural outcome of a strong brand footprint plus strong SEO.

## Strategic implications (how to optimize for AI)

- [ ] Increase brand mentions across reputable sources (PR, expert interviews, podcasts, third-party reviews, academic mentions).
- [ ] Increase high-authority backlinks that reinforce credibility.
- [ ] Create content with depth and uniqueness models can trust: expert/patient quotes, proprietary data and original research, structured elements (clear heading hierarchy, lists, comparison tables, timelines, FAQs), question-based subheadings, and answer-first summaries near the top of key pages.
- [ ] Strengthen traditional SEO across technical, content, and authority so URLs rise in search — and therefore in AI citations.
- [ ] For JS-heavy builders (Framer/Webflow hydration): remember LLMs generally don't execute JavaScript when crawling, so consider server-rendered content and a possible platform migration if AI visibility is a priority.

**Bottom line to close on:** keep investing in the fundamentals, and AI-answer presence compounds over time.
