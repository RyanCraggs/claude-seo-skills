## SLIDE 3 — Reporting Period
HEAD: We now report full calendar months
DECK: This report covers July 1 to 31. Previous reports ran mid-month to mid-month, which left every report with an incomplete final month.
- All comparisons are July against June
- June traffic reads 894 on the new basis

mid-May to mid-June. Calendar June is 894. Different windows, both correct.

## SLIDE 4 — The Headlines
HEAD: AI Overviews gained the most ground
DECK: Tracked-prompt performance improved on all three platforms. We gained on the prompts we manage and lost ground across the wider topic space.
- AI Overviews mentions +8.1%, position improved 1.6 to 1.3
- AI traffic 1,046 visits, +17%, with ChatGPT at 94%
- Cedar Valley Tech mentions +81, but eight of nine programs fell
- Bounce rate improved from 32% to 28%
NOTES: Lead with AI Overviews. It is the clearest evidence a prior recommendation worked, and it earns
attention for the two harder findings underneath.

## SLIDE 5 — The Prompts We Track
HEAD: Is this still the right prompt set?
DECK: We track 25 prompts across ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. That is 75 combinations measured daily.
- Six themes: location, surgical tech, online, allied health, trades, financial aid
- No prompts cover transfer or articulation pathways
- No prompts cover cost or tuition
NOTES: The agency contact asked for this slide. Don't rush it — it is designed to start a conversation.
Affordability is a top-five concept in AI answers about the college and we track nothing against it.

## SLIDE 6 — Visibility Overview: Definitions
HEAD: What we measure across all topics
DECK: These four metrics cover the whole topic space, not just the prompts we track.
- AI Visibility: a 0 to 100 composite, not a percentage
- Mentions: prompts that triggered a Cedar Valley Tech reference
- Monthly Audience: search volume of topics where we appear
- Cited Pages: our URLs used as sources in AI answers
NOTES: Reusable month to month. Worth keeping because the client asks what AI Visibility means roughly
every other meeting.

## SLIDE 7 — Visibility Overview: The Numbers
HEAD: Holding position in a shrinking conversation
DECK: Mention volume peaked in April and has fallen since. The composite score barely moved, which means competitors are declining alongside us.
- AI Visibility 34, against 39 in March and May
- Mentions 612, down from an April peak near 980
- Google AI Overviews supplies 251 of 550 attributed mentions
NOTES: Don't let 34 versus 39 become the headline. The relative story matters more, and the mention
decline runs across the topic set rather than being specific to us. The model bars attribute 550 of the
612 mentions on the card; the rest are unattributed in the tool.

## SLIDE 8 — The Divergence
HEAD: Two different pictures

- Most of the regional conversation sits outside our measurement
NOTES: This is the spine of the report. If they take one idea away, make it this one. It also makes the

## SLIDE 9 — Performance by Platform

DECK: Google AI Overviews improved most on both measures. Gemini holds the best position but is the only platform losing frequency.

- Gemini: best position at 1.14, mentions down 2.0%
- ChatGPT: flat frequency, position improved 1.40 to 1.19

AI Overviews our weakest platform and prescribed FAQs, location signals and schema. Claim the win.

## SLIDE 12 — Notable Declines
HEAD: Two apparent wins are actually losses
DECK: Position can improve for the wrong reason. A prompt that stops appearing on its weak days looks like it climbed.
- Medical coding online: position 2.9 to 1.0, days 13 to 3
- Online associates degrees: position 4.8 to 1.0, days 11 to 4
- Online college classes fell on both measures
- Community colleges in Ohio vanished from AI Overviews
NOTES: Appearing rarely at position 1 is worse than appearing constantly at position 3. This slide is
why we track both metrics, and it is the honest version of a report that could have claimed two extra wins.

## SLIDE 13 — The Online Cluster
HEAD: Online terms are still our soft spot
DECK: The seven online and flexible-learning prompts slipped while the rest of the set gained. The June recommendation to audit online courses has not been actioned.
- Cluster days with mention fell 1%, rest of set gained 2%
- ChatGPT down 4%, Gemini down 4%, AI Overviews up 8%
- Third consecutive report showing this pattern
NOTES: Frame as an unactioned recommendation, not a new discovery. Same finding, now with three months
of evidence. Modest decline, but the only theme moving consistently against us.

## SLIDE 14 — Program-Level Visibility
HEAD: The college gains, the programs don't
DECK: AI platforms name Cedar Valley Tech more often and more prominently. They name our individual programs less.
- Institution mentions +81, share of voice 60.1% to 63.4%
- Eight of nine tracked programs lost mentions
- Surgical tech worst hit: two programs down 14 and 8
- Only Veterinary Technology grew, up 11
NOTES: Most actionable finding in the report, and it produces a named target list. Three surgical tech
prompts still hold position 1 while both surgical programs lost mentions — recommended for the
subject, not named for the credential. One month is a thin base. Say so, and confirm in August.

## SLIDE 15 — AI Referral Traffic
HEAD: AI traffic up seventeen percent
DECK: 1,046 visits from AI platforms in July, against 894 in June. Engagement improved alongside volume.
- ChatGPT delivers 983 of 1,046 visits, or 94%
- Bounce rate improved from 32% to 28%
